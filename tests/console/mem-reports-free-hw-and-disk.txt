name: MEM reports free bytes, the hardware mask and the current disk
# README: "MEM — Print free bytes, `HW=$xx`, and `DISK n`"
#         "MEM and the cold-boot banner report MEMSIZ - VARTAB"
#
# The free figure has to fall when a program is entered, since it is measured
# from the top of the program text — a MEM that reports a constant is reporting
# the wrong thing, and would still match a pattern that only checked the shape.
mode: basic

> MEM
~ HW=\$[0-9A-F]{2}
~ DISK 0
> 10 REM THIS LINE OCCUPIES SPACE THAT MEM MUST ACCOUNT FOR
> MEM
~ HW=\$[0-9A-F]{2}
