name: F fills a range, including a single byte, and does not run away backwards
# README: "F addr addr byte — Fill memory range with a byte value"
#
# The reversed range is the one worth writing down. `F 1010 1000 CC` asks to
# fill from a higher address to a lower one, which is not a range at all. What
# it must not do is walk forward from 1010 until the address wraps — the
# guard bytes on either side are there to catch exactly that.
mode: monitor

> F 1000 1007 CC
> M 1000 1007
~ ^\.:1000 CC CC CC CC CC CC CC CC \.{8}$
> F 1100 1100 DD
> M 1100 1107
~ ^\.:1100 DD 00
> > 1200 01 02 03 04 05 06 07 08
> F 1207 1200 EE
> M 1200 1207
! ^\.:1200 EE EE EE EE EE EE EE EE
> M 1240 1247
! ^\.:1240 EE EE EE EE
