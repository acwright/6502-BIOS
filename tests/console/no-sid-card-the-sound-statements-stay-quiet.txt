name: with no SID card, VOL and SOUND return without an error
# README: "SID absent — Beep, SOUND, VOL, SidPlayNote, SidSilence silently
#          return"
#
# Sound is the one thing a program can lose and still be doing its job, which is
# why this row is silence rather than NO DEVICE: a game that beeps on a hit
# should keep playing on a machine with no sound card, not stop with an error on
# the line that made the noise.
#
# As with the video row, the statement still has to eat its arguments — a SOUND
# that returned before parsing would leave the rest of the line to be read as
# statements. The trailing PRINT on each line is what shows it did.
#
# The companion probe (no-sid-card-nothing-reaches-the-sound-chip) asserts the
# other half at the bus: quiet here means "no error", and it means "no writes"
# there. Neither alone is the promise.
mode: basic
hw: -sid

> VOL 9 : PRINT "AFTER VOL"
~ ^AFTER VOL$
> SOUND 1, 440, 1 : PRINT "AFTER SOUND"
~ ^AFTER SOUND$

# The documented ranges still hold with nothing fitted, exactly as they do for
# LOCATE and COLOR: a program that is wrong is wrong on every machine, and
# finding out only once a sound card is fitted is finding out too late.
> VOL 16
~ \?ILLEGAL QUANTITY ERROR
> SOUND 0, 440, 1
~ \?ILLEGAL QUANTITY ERROR
> SOUND 4, 440, 1
~ \?ILLEGAL QUANTITY ERROR

# And a program plays its way through to the end.
> 10 VOL 15
> 20 SOUND 1, 440, 1
> 30 SOUND 2, 880, 1
> 40 PRINT "RAN"
> RUN
~ ^RAN$
